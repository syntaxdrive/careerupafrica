DO'S AND DON'TS
DO
Use Navy Blue in every post (logo, headers, or backgrounds)
Use Teal or Orange for energy and action
Keep backgrounds simple - white, light blue, or navy
Use high contrast for readability
Stick to 3 colors max per post
Test on mobile first

DON'T
Use bright pink, purple, or red
Use more than 3 colors in one post
Use dark text on dark backgrounds
Use light text on light backgrounds
Use busy patterns or gradients everywhere
Forget your logo
